# The Whispering Cave -- world notes

A small one-shot, meant as the engine's own test fixture as much as a
game. Nothing here is parsed by the engine yet -- `world/*.md` is where
lore/flavor text for prompt-building will live once Phase 2 wires the AI
provider into the main scene loop (see PLAN.md section 4.2). For now it's
just reference notes for whoever writes the next scene.

## Setting

A single cave system in a nameless hillside, three generations of local
kids have dared each other to enter and none have found anything but wet
socks and disappointment. That is, until now.

## Tone

Terse, a little eerie, no gore. Short paragraphs -- this is read on an
e-ink screen a few lines at a time, not scrolled through on a phone.

## Hooks for a longer version

- The iron key (from `crawl_space`) could unlock more than one door in a
  longer cut of this game -- currently it's a straight shot to the ending.
- `ending_key`'s "real secret" is a placeholder for something an AI DM
  could improvise per-playthrough once Phase 2 lands, rather than a fixed
  hand-written reveal.
